# Valuation profiles v1

## Manufacturing — CATL

Model: DCF, with partial valuation permitted only when each missing method is
listed in the receipt. Required inputs are five years of revenue, EBIT, tax,
D&A, capex, working-capital changes, cash, debt and diluted shares; price and
market cap come from the canonical market receipt. Page-bound availability is
proven for several official facts, but not yet complete for every period.
WACC, terminal growth and forecast horizon require Park/analyst review.

## Consumer — Kweichow Moutai

Model: DCF plus historical PE and peer comparison. Compared with manufacturing,
the central drivers are volume, price/mix, channel inventory, cash conversion
and payout policy; capex is less explanatory. Financial-statement facts can be
page-bound, but current peer multiples and consensus inputs remain missing.
Park must decide the peer set and the analyst-approved premium/terminal-growth
framework.

## Bank — Ping An Bank

Manufacturing FCFF DCF is not applicable: net interest income, credit costs,
regulatory capital and distributable earnings drive value. Use a dividend
discount or residual-income model, with P/B as a relative cross-check. Required
inputs: CET1/total capital, RWA, ROE, NIM, credit-cost/NPL assumptions, payout,
book value and shares. Annual reports can provide these facts page-bound, but
the model needs analyst-reviewed capital, credit-cost and payout assumptions.
Park must approve model preference, peer bank set and provisional assumption
ranges before any target price can be surfaced.
